# Plan: $100/day (≈ $3,000/month) from the tools

Made 27 Oct 2026. Based on your answers:
- **Time:** 8–15 hours a week.
- **Budget:** $0.
- **Earning methods:** affiliate links, Rooh Sites web-design leads, and ads later. No paid upgrades.
- **Payments:** international payments already work.

## The honest starting point
- **Today:** about 15 tools and ~150 pages. Search Console showed about 300 impressions and a handful of clicks a week. Umami showed about 5 real visitors a week.
- **The target:** $3,000/month means roughly **1,000× today's traffic**, plus income attached to it.
- **Timeline:** realistic only over **12–18 months** of steady work, and not guaranteed. Many tool sites stall, so we measure monthly and double down on whatever works.
- **Ads alone won't get there.** At about $10 per 1,000 page views (mostly US traffic; India pays much less), $3,000/month from ads would need ~300,000 page views a month.

So the plan mixes three income sources, each paying differently per visitor.
