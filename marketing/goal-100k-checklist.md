## 6. Creator affiliate program for Slotsmith (Freemius)

**Settings to approve**
- Commission **30% lifetime** (40% for a few top creators through custom terms)
- Cookie **90 days**
- Credit **first referrer**
- Joining: **manual approval**; customers auto-approved
- Payouts: monthly, after the 14-day refund window; $100 minimum; paid by us through PayPal (Freemius does not pay affiliates)

**In plain words**
- A creator's link brings a Pro buyer ($79): the creator gets $23.70, and again every year the customer renews. Business ($149) pays $44.70, Agency ($299) pays $89.70.
- 90 days: a buyer who clicks the link and returns within 90 days still counts for the creator.
- First referrer: if two creators' links are clicked, the first one gets the credit.
- Manual approval: we check the channel or blog before accepting.
- A safer version, if you want it: 30% on the first year only.
