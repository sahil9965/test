## Accuracy rules for India pages
- **Lucky draws:** lotteries are regulated by Indian states. Frame the pages as event and fete coupons, never as "lottery" tickets. Tell organisers to check their local rules, and give no legal advice.
- **GST:** use only conservative statements, each checked before publishing:
  - a bill of supply is used by unregistered and composition suppliers;
  - exports of services under a LUT carry no IGST and need the export declaration line;
  - the registration threshold for services is ₹20 lakh in most states.
  - Link to the GST portal and say "check with your CA".
- **Influencer rates:** published ranges vary widely, so present no invented benchmarks; the calculator works from the creator's own numbers.
