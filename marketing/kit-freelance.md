# Freelance kit: Upwork, Contra, Codeable, portfolio and proposals

DRAFT, written 4 Nov 2026. Cash-flow work while the website packages get their first clients. Nothing here is posted; you create the accounts (they need your name, ID and payout details), then paste.

**Rules for every profile and proposal:** only claim what is true. No fake reviews, no invented clients, no made-up numbers. Sample sites are labelled as samples for made-up businesses. Say we are based in India and work remotely.

## 1. Upwork profile

**Title (70 characters or fewer):**
`WordPress booking, Google Business Profile and WhatsApp setup`

**Overview:**

> I help small service businesses get found and booked online.
>
> I build and fix WordPress sites, set up online booking (including payments and reminders), tidy up Google Business Profiles, and connect WhatsApp and email reminders so fewer appointments are missed.
>
> I also wrote Slotsmith, a WordPress appointment booking plugin (free on WordPress.org once approved, with a Pro version), including its documentation and test suite, and I run Rooh Sites, a small studio that builds websites and 15 free business tools used by salons, cafés, contractors and sellers.
>
> How I work: I reply within a day, I quote a fixed price before I start, I tell you plainly if something isn't worth doing, and I send a short written summary when I'm done. I work remotely from India and I'm comfortable with US, UK and Australian time zones for calls.
>
> Typical jobs: set up online booking for a salon, tutor or clinic · fix a broken or slow WordPress site · connect Stripe or PayPal to a booking form · set up WhatsApp or email appointment reminders · clean up a Google Business Profile · small WordPress plugin fixes.

**Skills to add:** WordPress, WordPress plugin development, PHP, JavaScript, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business API, Stripe, PayPal, Google Calendar API, appointment scheduling, website maintenance.

**Rate:** set a project-first approach: hourly rate $25 (so the profile is not empty), but propose **fixed prices** in every proposal.

**Portfolio entries (3):** see section 4.

**Do not** buy Connects bundles beyond the free monthly ones until the first two jobs are won.

## 5. Proposal templates

Rules: read the whole job post; mention one specific detail from it in the first line; fixed price; one question at the end; under 150 words. Replace the {braces}.

### A. Booking setup
> Hi {name}, you want {their goal in their words}. I can set that up on your existing WordPress site: services, staff hours, time zone, confirmation emails, and {payments / deposits / reminders if asked}, tested with a real booking before I hand it over.
>
> Fixed price: ${250 to 450 depending on payments and reminders}, ready in {3 to 5} days. I use Slotsmith, a booking plugin I wrote, so I can fix any problem fast. If a free option fits you better, I'll tell you.
>
> One question: do customers pay when they book, or at the appointment?

### B. WordPress fix or speed-up
> Hi {name}, about {the specific symptom}. Before quoting a fix I'd look at {first two things to check}, which usually takes 30 minutes.
>
> Fixed price for diagnosis plus fix: ${150 to 300}, and if it turns out to be bigger I'll tell you the price before doing more. Backup first, and I send a short written summary of what changed.
>
> Can you share a temporary admin login (or screen-share) so I can look today?

### C. Google Business Profile cleanup
> Hi {name}, I'll go through your Google Business Profile and fix what hurts you most: website link, categories, hours, services, photos, and a first post, then send you a short written list of what I changed and what to do next.
>
> Fixed price: ${150 to 300}, done in {2 to 3} days. I don't promise rankings (nobody honestly can), but a complete profile gets more calls.
>
> Do you have Manager access to the profile, or does someone else?

### D. WhatsApp or email reminders
> Hi {name}, to cut missed appointments I'd set up automatic reminders {24 hours / 2 hours before} by email and WhatsApp, with an approved message template, and test them on your own phone.
>
> Fixed price: ${150 to 350}, ready in {3 to 5} days. WhatsApp needs a Meta business account and template approval, which can add a day or two outside my control; I'll guide you through it.
>
> Which numbers do you want reminders to come from?

## 6. Weekly routine (2 to 3 hours a day goes to sales and outreach in total; freelance gets part of it)
- Mon, Wed, Fri: search Upwork for new posts with the keywords *WordPress booking*, *appointment plugin*, *Google Business Profile*, *WhatsApp reminders*; send up to 10 proposals a week, best-fit first.
- Log every proposal on the checklist scoreboard: sent, replies, calls, wins, dollars.
- First two jobs: price at the low end to earn reviews; do the work properly; ask for an honest review at the end (any rating), never a scripted one.
- Never move a client off-platform before the platform's rules allow it.
