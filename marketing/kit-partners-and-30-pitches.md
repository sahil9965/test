## 1. The white-label offer (for local SEO consultants and small agencies)

**What it is:** they sell websites to their local clients under their own name; we build them, in their brand, behind the scenes. They keep the client relationship and the margin.

**Partner price list (DRAFT: please approve or change the numbers):**

| Package we build | Their client pays (their choice; our list price shown) | Partner price to us | Their margin at list |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard, up to 6 pages | $599 | $300 | $299 |
| Launch, 5 pages + Google listing cleanup + contact form or booking | $1,500 | **$600** (the figure in the plan) | $900 |
| Growth, up to 10 pages + booking with deposits and reminders | $3,000 | $1,200 | $1,800 |
| Care plan, per client per month | $99 / $199 | $50 / $100 | $49 / $99 |

*These partner prices are my proposal; only the $600 for Launch came from the plan.* Terms to match: 50% to start, 50% before launch; partner supplies the client's photos, text and logo (or pays our hourly rate to chase them); 2 rounds of changes per site; we never contact their client directly without their say; we sign a simple "no poaching, no names" note (we do not show the work as ours unless they allow it).

**Why it's useful to them (true things only):** no hiring or freelancer risk; fixed prices and dates (first draft in 5 business days after we have the material; live in 14 to 20 business days depending on package); the sites are fast, phone-first and include the search basics (titles, descriptions, sitemap); we work in writing, so their time zone is fine.

**What we should have ready before the first call:** two sample sites to show (the sample designs are for made-up businesses; label them as such), the trade pages, one reference-style message from the first proof client once we have one, a one-page partner sheet (this table, delivery times, what each package includes, what we need from the partner).
