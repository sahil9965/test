# Proof-client kit: the first three clients

DRAFT, written 4 Nov 2026. Goal: three real Launch sites, each with an honest testimonial, a case study (with written permission) and a referral, so every later pitch has proof. After the third, raise prices about 30%.

## The deal
The first 3 clients get the **Launch package** (list price $1,500) for about **$800**. We ask three things in return, and none depends on them liking us:
1. **Honest feedback** at the end, and permission to publish it (any rating; we do not script or edit the meaning).
2. **A case study** about their project, published only with their written permission (you can read it first and cut anything).
3. **A referral**: an introduction to one business owner who might want a site, if they are happy to give one.

**Fairness and the law:** because the discount is linked to feedback, we say so on the published testimonial ("Received a discounted rate for being one of our first clients") and we never ask for a positive review or condition the discount on sentiment. This matches the FTC's rules on endorsements and reviews.

## Who to offer it to (in this order)
1. Warm contacts: businesses you already know, people who replied to the robot's emails ("talking" status), anyone who booked the 15-minute call.
2. Owners of local businesses in the towns the robot covers who have no website or a dead link (verified).
3. Friends-of-friends via Facebook groups for salon, barber and tutor owners.

Pick businesses that will be easy to work with and **look good in a case study**: a clear service, good photos, an owner who answers within a day. Avoid anyone who wants a logo, a shop, or ten pages.

## One-page agreement (plain English; keep under one page)
- **What you get:** the Launch package as listed on roohsites.com. Price: $800, 50% to start, 50% before launch.
- **What we need from you:** photos, text, opening hours, logo (if you have one), and answers within 2 business days.
- **Timeline:** first draft in about 5 business days after we have your material; live in about 14 business days.
- **Feedback and case study:** at the end you give honest feedback. We may publish it and a short case study **only after you approve the exact words in writing**. You can say no to any part, at any time, at no cost.
- **Logo and name:** we may show your business name and logo as a client only if you say yes in the permission form below.
- **Discount disclosure:** published feedback will say you received a discounted rate.
- **Your ownership:** the domain is in your name; the finished site is yours; we hand over a copy of the files if you ask.
- **If we cannot deliver what we agreed:** we refund what you paid for the work not delivered.
- Signed by both (an email reply of "I agree" is fine).

**Permission form (separate, signed after launch):** tick boxes for: publish my feedback · publish the case study · show my business name · show my logo · show screenshots of my site · allow a link to my site · allow my name and role. Date and signature.

## Questions to ask at the start (discovery, 20 minutes)
1. What do you do, and who are your best customers? 2. What do you want a visitor to do (call, book, message, visit)? 3. What three questions do customers always ask? 4. What do competitors do that you like or dislike? 5. Which photos do you have, and which do you need? 6. How do you take bookings today? 7. Anything you do not want on the site? 8. What would make this a success for you in 3 months? (Write the answer down: it goes in the case study.)

## Case study template (about 300 words; every number must be real)
- **The business:** name, town, what they do.
- **The problem, in their words:** (their quote from the discovery call).
- **What we did:** pages, Google Business Profile changes, booking or form, anything special.
- **The result:** only what we can show: live date, days from start to live, what they said. Add numbers (calls, bookings, views) **only** if the client shares them and agrees, with dates. No invented percentages.
- **Quote:** their approved words, with the discount disclosure line.
- **Screenshots:** phone and desktop, with their permission.
- **Link** to the live site (with permission).

## Testimonial request (send at launch + 7 days, not before)
> Hi {Name}, thank you again for being one of our first clients. If you're willing, would you reply with a few honest lines about working with us: what went well, what didn't, and what you'd change? I'll only publish it with your approval, and it can be critical. If you'd prefer not to, that's absolutely fine.

## Referral ask (only when they've said they're happy)
> {Name}, it's great the site is live. Is there one other business owner you know who might also want a site? You're under no obligation; if you do know someone, an intro by email is all I need, and I'll take it from there.
