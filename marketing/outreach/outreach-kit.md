# Outreach kit: first web-design clients (US small businesses)

Goal: 15 personal emails a week to small businesses that need a better website, leading with something free and useful from our tools. Time: about 3–4 hours a week.

Honest expectations: most emails get no reply, and that's normal for cold outreach. Plan on 4–8 weeks of steady sending before the first client, and treat every reply as a win you can learn from.

---

## 0. One-time setup (about 30 minutes)

### A Rooh Sites email address
Send from **hello@roohsites.com**, not Gmail and not hello@halosell.com. A cold email can draw spam complaints, and those would hurt Halosell's mail reputation. Free options:
- **Zoho Mail free plan** (one custom domain, web and app only), if it's still offered for your region. It sends and receives as hello@roohsites.com. Add the MX, SPF and DKIM records it gives you in Cloudflare DNS (roohsites.com is already on Cloudflare).
- Or **Cloudflare Email Routing** (free) to receive at hello@roohsites.com in your Gmail, plus Gmail's "Send mail as" to reply from that address. Easier, but mail sent this way is less likely to reach inboxes. Use it only if Zoho isn't available.

Then send yourself a test at a Gmail address and check it isn't in spam.

### The rules we follow (US CAN-SPAM; the UK, Canada and Australia are different)
Every outreach email must:
1. Come from your real name and address, with an honest subject line (no fake "Re:" or "Fwd:").
2. Say clearly that it's a business offer. Our signature line does this.
3. Include a **valid physical postal address**. Your current street address is fine, including your address in India. A PO box only counts if it's a US Postal Service box or a private mailbox registered with a mail-receiving agency.
4. Tell them how to stop emails. Honour every "no" or "stop" at once, and never email them again (the law gives you 10 business days; we do it the same day).

Where we send:
- **United States:** yes, business email addresses only.
- **United Kingdom:** only to limited companies (names ending "Ltd" or "LLP"; check on Companies House). Sole traders and most partnerships need consent first, so skip them, and skip any business you can't confirm is a company. The ICO says you can't tell from an email address alone.
- **Canada and Australia:** skip for now. Their laws need consent first.

Never buy email lists, never use bulk-sending tools, no tracking pixels, no attachments in the first email.

Sources: [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), [ICO: business-to-business marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/).

## 1. Who to contact

Start with **one niche for two weeks**, so you learn what works faster. Recommended first niche: **salons and spas**. The holiday gift season is about to start, and GiftStub makes their gift certificates.

| Niche | Free thing to offer (made with our tools in about 2 minutes) |
|---|---|
| Salons, barbers, nail studios, spas | Numbered holiday gift certificates with their name ([GiftStub](https://giftcert.roohsites.com)) |
| Cafés, bakeries, car washes, dog groomers | A loyalty punch card with their name and colours ([PunchPerk](https://punchcard.roohsites.com)) |
| Electricians | A neat panel schedule template ([PanelDex](https://panelschedule.roohsites.com)) |
| Contractors, cleaners, handymen | A clean invoice template with their details ([InvoiceKit](https://invoice.roohsites.com)) |

### A good target has all of these
- At least **4.3 stars and 20+ reviews** on Google Maps, so it's a real, busy business that can afford $299.
- **No website**, only a Facebook page, or a site that's clearly broken, old or hard to use on a phone.
- A **business email** you can find: on their Facebook page (About), Instagram bio, or their own site's contact page.

Skip chains and franchises, businesses with a modern site already, and anyone whose page says "no solicitation".

### How to find them (free)
1. Google Maps: search `salon in [town], [state]`. Mid-size towns (50k–300k people) are less flooded by agencies than big cities.
2. Open each listing. If there's no website button, or it goes to Facebook, open that page and look for an email.
3. Add the business to the tracker (section 4). Aim for 15 a week.

## 2. Email templates

Before sending, **make the free item first** (it takes about 2 minutes with the tool), so "I made" is true. Then send the PDF only when they reply "yes". Asking first keeps you out of spam folders and starts a conversation.

Keep every email under about 120 words, plain text, with one link (roohsites.com). Change at least one line so it's genuinely about them.

### Email 1: no website (salon example)
**Subject:** Holiday gift certificates for [Business]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I came across [Business] on Google Maps. [Rating] stars from [count] reviews is great work.
>
> I noticed you don't have a website yet, so people comparing salons in [Town] only see your photos and reviews. I run Rooh Sites, a small studio that builds simple, fast websites for salons and shops: from $299 one-time, or $39 a month with hosting and updates included. Examples and prices: roohsites.com
>
> To say hello, I made a set of numbered holiday gift certificates with [Business]'s name on them, ready to print. Want me to send the PDF? It's yours either way.
>
> [Your name]
> Rooh Sites · roohsites.com
> [Postal address]
> This is a one-time business offer. Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.

### Email 1: site exists but is weak
**Subject:** An idea for [Business]'s website

> Hi [Name],
>
> I found [Business] on Google Maps. [Rating] stars from [count] reviews is impressive.
>
> I had a look at your website on my phone, and [one specific, kind observation: "the booking button is hard to find" / "the text is tiny on a phone" / "the opening hours look out of date"]. A lot of your customers will see it on a phone first.
>
> I run Rooh Sites, a small studio that builds clean, fast websites for local businesses: from $299 one-time, or $39 a month all-in. Examples: roohsites.com
>
> I also made you a [punch card / set of gift certificates] with your name on it. Want me to send it over? Free either way.
>
> [Signature block as above]

### Email 1: trades (electrician example)
**Subject:** Panel schedule template for [Business]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I came across [Business] on Google Maps. [count] reviews at [rating] stars says a lot.
>
> We make a free panel schedule and breaker label tool for electricians (panelschedule.roohsites.com), and I set one up with your company name on it. Want the PDF?
>
> We also build simple websites for trades: your services, areas you cover, past jobs and a one-tap call button. From $299 one-time, or $39 a month. Examples: roohsites.com
>
> [Signature block]

### Follow-up 1: 4 days later, same thread
**Subject:** (keep their thread)

> Hi [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried. The [gift certificates / punch card] for [Business] are ready whenever you want them, no catch.
>
> [Your name]
> Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.

### Follow-up 2: a week after that, then stop
> Hi [Name], last note from me. If a website ever moves up your list, roohsites.com has prices and examples, and I'm happy to answer questions. Wishing you a busy holiday season!
>
> [Your name]

After follow-up 2, stop. No reply means no.

### Facebook or Instagram message (when there's no email)
> Hi! I came across [Business] on Google Maps, and your reviews are great. I made a set of [gift certificates / a punch card] with your name on it, free. Want me to send it? I also build simple websites for small businesses (roohsites.com), but no pressure at all.

## 3. Answering replies

| They say | You reply (short and friendly) |
|---|---|
| "Yes, send it" | Send the PDF. Add: "If you'd like a website that shows your services and lets people book, I can sketch a homepage idea for free. Just send a few photos." |
| "How much?" | "From $299 one-time for 1–3 pages, $599 for up to 6 pages, or $39 a month with domain, hosting and updates included. Full details: roohsites.com/#pricing" |
| "How does it work?" | "You tell us about the business and send photos. We send a first draft in about a week, you ask for changes, then we go live. It all happens by email or WhatsApp. If you'd like to talk it through, pick a time here: cal.com/roohsites/15min" |
| "We use Facebook" | "Facebook is great for regulars. A website is what new customers find on Google, and you own it. Happy to show you an example for your trade." |
| "Where are you based?" | "We're a small studio based in India and work with clients by email and WhatsApp. Calls happen at a time that suits you, in your time zone." |
| "Not now" | "No problem at all. I won't follow up. Good luck this season!" Mark them **Not now** and don't email again. |
| "Stop" / "Unsubscribe" / anything rude | Reply nothing, or "Removed, sorry for the bother." Mark them **Opted out** the same day. Never contact them again. |

When someone is interested, send them the booking link, **https://cal.com/roohsites/15min** (a 15-minute call), or point them to the roohsites.com contact form or WhatsApp. Form enquiries land in your dashboard Inbox.

## 4. Tracking

Import `tracker.csv` (in this folder) into Google Sheets. One row per business. Status values:
`Found → Sent → Follow-up 1 → Follow-up 2 → Replied → Call → Won` or `Not now` / `Opted out` / `No reply`.

Rules:
- An **Opted out** row is never deleted. It's the do-not-contact list.
- Before adding a new business, search the sheet so you never email anyone twice.
