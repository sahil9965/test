## 3. All the income options (the full menu)

**Services**
- Websites with booking, packages **$1,200–$3,500** (Launch, Growth) plus a care plan. Freelance US small-business sites are quoted at $2,000–$8,000, with a median of $3,000–$5,000 ([Levitate](https://www.levitate.ai/blog-posts/average-website-design-cost-for-small-businesses-in-2026), [Jim.com](https://jim.com/blog/small-business-website-cost)).
- **Care plans:** basic $30–$100, standard $100–$300 a month, full service $435+ ([Fatlab](https://fatlabwebsupport.com/blog/website-maintenance/wordpress-care-plan-pricing/), [Seahawk](https://seahawkmedia.com/wordpress/wordpress-website-care-plans-guide/)). White-label care providers resell for about $39 a site per month, so you can sell under your brand with a margin.
- **Google Business Profile and local SEO** setup and monthly management.
- **AI and WhatsApp automation** for small businesses: booking reminders, quote bots, lead follow-up.
- **Plugin setup and customisation** for Slotsmith customers ($297 setup service).
- **Freelance platforms** (Upwork, Contra, Codeable) for fast cash while the others ramp.

**Partnerships and sales force**
- **White-label** for agencies and local SEO consultants: they sell, you build for $500–$900 a site.
- **Commission-only sales partners** for web design: pay 20–30% of each project to people who bring clients.
- **Referral rewards** for happy clients ($100–$250 per referred project).
- **Sell the lead robot as a service** to agencies (a monthly fee for leads and sample sites).

**Slotsmith**
- **Pro, Business and Agency** licences ($79 / $149 / $299 a year).
- **Creator affiliate program** through Freemius: 30% lifetime recommended (see the affiliate section below).
- **Lifetime-deal marketplaces** such as AppSumo. Listing is free, revenue share is negotiated, payouts are Net 60, and the listing must stay up 120 days. Deals usually carry deep discounts and acceptance is competitive ([AppSumo terms](https://appsumo.com/partner-terms/payment-policy), [Freemius on AppSumo](https://freemius.com/blog/appsumo-marketplace-breakdown/)). Good for launch cash, with the risk that it reduces yearly renewals. Apply at sell.appsumo.com.
- **Bundles:** website + Slotsmith + setup for salons and tutors.
- **Add-ons later:** SMS credits, more integrations, premium form templates.

**Lead generation for trades**
- Cost per lead for service trades is quoted at **$40–$150**, and monthly retainers at **$2,000–$6,000** including ad spend, often as a base plus a performance bonus ([ClicksGeek](https://clicksgeek.com/lead-generation-services-pricing/), [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/lead-generation-pricing)). We can start with a pilot for two contractors who fund the ads.

**Tools**
- **Paid unlocks** ($9–$29 one-time) on the tools with real traffic: ABA, homeschool transcripts, FNSKU, panel schedules, the estimate maker.
- **Templates** on Etsy, Teachers Pay Teachers and Gumroad.
- **Amazon** links on tool pages only (printers, label rolls, card stock), with the disclosure. Never in emails or PDFs.
- **Ads** once a tool site passes about 10,000 page views a month.

## 6. Creator affiliate program for Slotsmith
Freemius has a built-in affiliate platform: referral links, visit and sale tracking, and a dashboard for each creator. Freemius does **not** pay affiliates; we pay once a month through PayPal, after the 10th ([Freemius docs](https://freemius.com/help/documentation/affiliate-platform/paying-affiliates-commission/)).

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Commission | 30% lifetime (40% for a few top creators) |
| Cookie | 90 days |
| Credit | First referrer |
| Joining | Manual approval; auto-approve for customers |
| Payouts | Monthly, after the 14-day refund window; $100 minimum |

At 30%, a creator earns about $23.70 on Pro, $44.70 on Business and $89.70 on Agency per sale, again on each renewal. Still to do: test with a second email, ask the CA and PayPal how to pay creators abroad, and build the /affiliates page, terms and creator kit.
