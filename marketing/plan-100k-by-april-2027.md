## 2. Market study (what the numbers say)

Sources are blogs and vendors, so treat every figure as a rough benchmark. Links are at the bottom.

- **Web design prices (US, 2026).** A freelancer's small-business site is quoted at **$2,000–$8,000**, with a median of **$3,000–$5,000** for 5–10 pages. Agencies: **$5,000–$25,000**. DIY builders: $200–$1,000. Upkeep: about **$35–$100 a month**, managed plans **$249–$1,750+ a month**. Our $299 / $599 prices sit at the DIY end of the market.
- **Cold email.** The most quoted 2026 reply rate is about **3.4%** (other sources: 0.7–5.8%). One agency funnel: reply about 10%, positive reply 2.2%, meetings 1.2%, opportunities 0.6%. **Your October data: 7 replies and 3 meetings from 49 sends**, much better than average, but the sample is tiny and no deal has closed.
- **AdSense.** Page RPM = earnings per 1,000 page views. Blog estimates for US traffic range from **$5–$15** (mid-range sites) to $20–$50 (finance niches). No source covers utility sites. Plan on **$5–$10**.
- **Amazon Associates.** Commission rates for relevant categories (electronics, home improvement) are quoted at **about 1–4.5%**, and one source says rates fell in 18 of 27 categories since 2020. Office products: unclear. A $200 thermal printer pays about $2–$9.
- **Freemium conversion.** Typical free-to-paid for SaaS: **2–5%** (3.7% average in one study; small-business tools quoted higher). These figures are not for plugins or single-purpose tools, so treat them as an upper bound for us.
- **WordPress affiliate programs.** Most pay roughly 30% and many are recurring, but I could not verify any current rate (Hostinger, Elementor, Cloudways, Kinsta, WPForms). Check each program's page.

## 7. My opinions

1. **Stop building new free tools after the contractor estimate maker.** 17 sites with about 24 search clicks a month is the signal: the bottleneck is getting visitors and clients, not more tools.
2. **Your idea ranking, by money by April:** web clients (high) > paid features in busy tools (medium, traffic-gated) > plugin affiliate (low) > Amazon (low) > AdSense (very low until 10,000+ monthly page views) > "increase traffic" (essential, but it pays in 6–12 months).
3. **Price is the lever, not volume.** $299 sites cannot reach a six-figure number. The same sample-site machine at $1,500–$3,500 can.
4. **Use Slotsmith as the product for one vertical:** "website + online booking + WhatsApp reminders" for salons, tutors and clinics. It is the reason to charge $1,500+ instead of $299.
5. **Plugin affiliate** has two meanings, and the better one is **your own** affiliate program for Slotsmith through Freemius. Recommending other products pays little unless it's hosting or recurring SaaS.
6. **Build proof before volume.** Three real case studies are worth more than 3,000 more emails.
7. **Protect cash.** If November closes nothing, add freelance gigs early. Don't wait for the tools to pay.
