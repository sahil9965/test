# Short videos: 6 scripts (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels)

Each video is a 20–30 second **screen recording of the tool doing its job**, with big text on screen. No face, no voice needed (add a voice-over only if you like). Post one a day; when all 6 are up, record them again with the alternative hooks.

## Setup (once, 15 minutes)
- **Record vertical (9:16).** On iPhone, open the tool in Safari or Chrome and use Control Centre → Screen Recording. On a computer, make the browser window narrow (about phone width), record it, and crop to 9:16 in the editor.
- **Edit in CapCut (free) or the Instagram/YouTube editor.** Add the on-screen text from the script, cut the waiting bits, and add quiet music from the app's free library.
- **Before you record:** try the steps once, so the recording has no pauses. Use made-up example names, never a real customer's.
- **Instagram bio link:** tools.roohsites.com (all 15 tools). Then "link in bio" works for every video.
- **Links in descriptions:** use the links given below. They carry tracking tags, so Umami shows which videos send visitors.
- **Post the same file to all three:** YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels.

Times are approximate; shorter is fine.
