### GlanceSheet (IEP at a Glance maker) — build
Fill in a short form and get a printable one-page 'IEP at a Glance' or student snapshot for gen-ed teachers, subs and paras. It includes a picklist of common accommodations and a class-wide accommodations matrix, and everything stays on the device.

- **Audience:** US special education case managers, co-teachers, gen-ed teachers with students on IEPs or 504 plans, school psychologists, SLPs
- **Keywords:** iep at a glance template; iep at a glance template editable free, iep snapshot template, student at a glance template, accommodations at a glance chart for teachers, 504 plan at a glance template, iep accommodations checklist for general education teachers
- **What we'd do better:** A real generator rather than a file to copy. It would offer a picklist of about 60 common accommodations grouped by category (presentation, response, setting, timing, behavior), short goal-area summaries, services and minutes, strengths and interests, triggers and what helps, and contacts. Outputs: (a) a polished one-page snapshot per student, (b) a caseload batch print, and (c) a class 'accommodations matrix' (students × accommodations grid) for each gen-ed teacher. Data never leaves the browser, which is a real selling point for FERPA-anxious teachers. A backup file can be reloaded next year.
- **Output:** A one-page IEP at a Glance PDF per student, a batch PDF for a whole caseload, a class accommodations matrix PDF, a JSON backup file, and the 'Made with' credit in the footer
- **Fits the network:** Strong with GoalTally, which serves the same special education audience (data sheets and graphs), so the two can cross-link. Moderate with BellBook (school staff).
- **Reviewer's call:** Spam farms hold the whole head-term page 1, which shows real search volume and no good answer, and the proposal is a real generator (caseload batch printing plus an accommodations matrix), which is clearly better than any static file. It shares an audience with GoalTally, and the local-only privacy story is a real advantage. Adoption risk: some districts ban unapproved web tools for student data, so the copy has to say plainly that nothing is uploaded.
- **First pages to build:** iep at a glance template (free, editable, printable); iep snapshot template; 504 plan at a glance template; student accommodations matrix template for gen-ed teachers; iep accommodations checklist for general education teachers; student at a glance template for subs and paras
- **Risks:** Student personal information means no field may ever reach analytics or a server, and that has to be stated clearly. Copy must make clear it is a summary and not the legal IEP. Teach Starter and TEA have strong domain authority, and TPT sells many paid versions that do well on Pinterest. Use is seasonal (back to school, plus IEP updates), so repeat visits are moderate.
- **Evidence checked:** Search 1, the head term 'iep at a glance template': all 9 page-1 results were spam mirror pages built for that exact title: https://edge-eu-west-2.betteruptime.com/en/iep-at-a-glance-template.html, https://get.cloudshark.org/view/iep-at-a-glance-template.html, https://msoid.icmbio.gov.br/templates/iep-at-a-glance-template.html, https://live.museum.manchester.ac.uk/library/iep-at-a-glance-template.html, https://chw.dhis2.org/template/iep-at-a-glance-template.html, https://lbas.lboro.ac.uk/viewer/iep-at-a-glance-template.html, https://lbas.lboro.ac.uk/template/iep-at-a-glance-template.html, https://track.ctrader.com/viewer/iep-at-a-glance-template.html, https://phpmyadmin.muycomputerpro.com/viewer/iep-at-a-glance-template.html. No real publisher showed up. Search 2, 'iep at a glance generator student snapshot tool for teachers': more spam (chw.dhis2.org, msoid.icmbio.gov.br free-iep-at-a-glance-template, lbas.lboro.ac.uk/template/iep-snapshot-template-editable.html, track.ctrader.com) plus three blog posts: https://eunasolutions.com/?p=2932 (IEP Summary Template from a vendor), https://www.mrsdscorner.com/?p=13220 and https://positivelylearningblog.com/?p=23736 (posts on IEP snapshots). I found no generator. Earlier evidence from the proposer: Teach Starter, the Texas Education Agency (TEA) and SpedTrack offer only static or email-gated files. I did not check TPT, Reddit or AI IEP tools.

### SubReady (sub binder and emergency sub plan generator) — build
Fill in your class info once (schedule, rosters, seating chart, procedures, duties, helpful students, emergency info) and print a complete, consistent sub binder, plus a one-page 'sub plans at a glance' you can refresh in seconds.

- **Audience:** US K-12 classroom teachers, special education teachers and paras, new teachers, instructional coaches building a school-wide sub binder
- **Keywords:** sub binder template editable free; sub binder template, free sub binder template, emergency sub plans template, substitute teacher information sheet, sub plan template editable, substitute folder template
- **What we'd do better:** A form-driven generator in place of a 40-page Slides file to retype every year. Sections are switchable (welcome letter, daily and A/B schedule, roster with photo-free seating chart, procedures, duties, behavior system, helpful students, special education and paras notes, emergency drills, tech logins redacted by default). It prints as one tidy binder PDF or individual pages, saves to a local backup file, and reuses the data next year. The one-page 'today at a glance' printout is quick to refresh before an absence.
- **Output:** A complete sub binder PDF, a one-page sub plan at-a-glance sheet, a printable seating chart, a JSON backup for reuse, and the 'Made with' credit in the footer
- **Fits the network:** Strong with BellBook, which already handles A/B-day schedules, so its schedule data could import into the sub binder, and both serve the same teachers. Moderate with GoalTally (special education teachers need sub notes for paras).
- **Reviewer's call:** Two head-ish terms each have a page 1 made of spam plus static PowerPoint files, so the SERP is very winnable, and every US teacher needs a sub binder each year. Repeat use is low, so its value rests on SEO and Pinterest, and on reusing BellBook schedule data. The scope should be the binder and the at-a-glance sheet, not AI-written lesson content.
- **First pages to build:** sub binder template (free, editable); emergency sub plans template; substitute teacher information sheet; one-page sub plan at a glance template; sub binder checklist; special education sub binder (para schedule and notes)
- **Risks:** Only one search, so demand and competition confidence are lower. TPT freebies and WeAreTeachers have strong Pinterest and brand presence. Use is mostly yearly, with occasional updates, so repeat visits are limited. Medical and emergency notes about students must stay local and be framed as teacher notes, not medical guidance. Teachers expect cute design.
- **Evidence checked:** I ran 2 more searches to cover the proposal's single search. 'sub binder template' (head term): 6 of 9 results were spam: https://msoid.icmbio.gov.br/templates/sub-binder-template.html, https://live.museum.manchester.ac.uk/library/sub-binder-template.html, https://edge-eu-west-2.betteruptime.com/en/free-sub-binder-template.html, https://chw.dhis2.org/template/free-sub-binder-template.html, https://msoid.icmbio.gov.br/templates/free-sub-binder-template.html, https://chw.dhis2.org/template/sub-binder-template-free.html. The only real results were static files: https://teachstarter.com/us/teaching-resource/sub-folder-sub-binder-template-pack and https://www.tes.com/en-ca/teaching-resource/editable-substitute-plan-template-quick-and-easy-prep-12687886 (pptx, retyped by hand). 'emergency sub plans template editable free': all 9 results were spam mirrors (msoid.icmbio.gov.br sub-plans-template, emergency-sub-plans-template and emergency-sub-plan-template; live.museum.manchester.ac.uk x3; lbas.lboro.ac.uk; track.ctrader.com x2). ### PunchPerk — build
Printable loyalty punch card maker. You set the business name, number of punches, reward and stamp icon, and it prints 10 business-card-size cards per sheet with optional serial numbers.

- **Audience:** Coffee carts, food trucks, salons, barbers, car washes, home bakers, dog groomers, smoothie and boba stands. Secondary audience: teachers making classroom behavior punch cards.
- **Keywords:** printable punch card template; free printable punch card template, editable punch card template, punch card template free, loyalty punch card template printable, coffee punch card template, salon loyalty card template
- **What we'd do better:** Real card sizes: 3.5x2 in, printing 10-up on Letter with Avery 8371/5371-style alignment, plus an aligned back side for duplex printing. You choose 5-12 punch circles or stamp icons and a reward line. It can add serial numbers or a small owner initial box to deter copying. It ships presets for coffee, salon, car wash, bakery and classroom, with one landing page each. It downloads a PDF with no login.
- **Output:** Print-ready PDF (front plus optional back, 10-up) and a PNG preview; small 'Made with PunchPerk' credit on the sheet margin, not on the cards
- **Fits the network:** TicketRun (numbering and multi-up card layout). The classroom behavior punch card pages reach the teacher audience of BellBook and GoalTally.
- **Reviewer's call:** This is a common, recurring template need for two audiences we can reach: small food and beauty businesses, and teachers making behavior or reading punch cards, which do well on Pinterest. In my checks page 1 was nearly all spam plus single-card editors. A no-login generator that prints 10 cards per sheet with duplex backs and serial numbers is visibly better, and it is a small build on TicketRun's layout code.
- **First pages to build:** coffee punch card template (10 per page, printable PDF); behavior punch card printable for the classroom; punch card template that prints 10 per sheet, sized for Avery 8371/5371 business-card stock (say 'compatible with' only; no Avery logo); salon / barber loyalty punch card template; editable punch card template free (no Canva, no login); chore / reading punch card for kids
- **Risks:** Checked with only one search, so confidence is lower. Canva, Avery and print shops (Vistaprint) likely rank for the shorter head terms. Customers reprint occasionally rather than weekly. Home-printed cards are easy to copy, so the tool must not promise fraud protection.
- **Evidence checked:** Three searches. (1) 'punch card template printable': all 10 results were spam mirror pages (my-test.valpo.edu, msoid.icmbio.gov.br x2, edge-eu-west-2.betteruptime.com, wrdsclassroom.wharton.upenn.edu, get.cloudshark.org, usa8-dev1.alpinelinux.org, lbas.lboro.ac.uk, track.ctrader.com x2). No real tool appeared. (2) 'coffee punch card template free': www.template.net/editable/71988/punch-card (a static Word/Docs template), and the rest were spam (webmail.ujcm.edu.pe, zim.dhis2.org, weee.roadtech.co.uk). (3) 'free punch card maker online printable loyalty card generator': templated.io/tools/free-loyalty-card-maker (a free generator that downloads one card as an image, not a print sheet), musthavemenus.com/feature/create-loyalty-cards.html (an editor tied to ordering prints) and fotor.com/design/business-card/loyalty-cards (a design editor), plus spam. I did not check whether templated.io needs a login, and I did not check Canva, Avery or Vistaprint on Google.

### LineupFair — build
Youth baseball and softball fielding rotation generator. Enter the roster and number of innings, and get a fair inning-by-inning defensive chart: no one sits twice before everyone sits once, everyone gets infield time, and you can lock pitcher and catcher. Prints as a dugout card PDF.

- **Audience:** Volunteer coaches and team parents in US recreational youth baseball, softball and t-ball (ages 5-12), where leagues often require roughly equal play and position variety
- **Keywords:** baseball fielding rotation generator; little league position rotation template, baseball defensive lineup template, tball positions template, baseball fielding lineup template, softball lineup card printable, baseball diamond lineup template
- **What we'd do better:** A real constraint-based generator rather than a blank grid. Rules: sit-out fairness, a minimum number of infield innings, no repeated position in a row, locked or excluded positions per player (for example 'not catcher'), and handling for late arrivals and absences. Batting order rotation would be free too. Outputs: a one-page dugout card PDF, a per-inning diamond view, and a season fairness tally that can be saved locally or carried in the URL. Landing pages could cover t-ball, coach pitch, 10-player outfields, softball, 'equal playing time' and a printable blank lineup card.
- **Output:** Dugout lineup card PDF (letter, plus an index-card size), a per-inning field diagram PNG and a season playing-time summary PDF, each with a small 'Made with LineupFair' credit
- **Fits the network:** Shares the coach and team-parent audience with TicketRun (team fundraisers) and with SnackShift below. Cross-link 'team season kit' pages.
- **Reviewer's call:** Demand is well evidenced: at least 5 apps solve this, farms target many lineup-template variants, and coaches repeat the job every game, 2-3 times a week. The web SERP has apps and farms but no free no-login in-browser generator, and the constraint solver plus dugout-card PDF runs fully client-side. The real threats are GameChanger's new lineup feature and DugoutIQ (unverified), and it is October, so launching now gives pages time to index before the Feb-June peak.
- **First pages to build:** baseball fielding rotation generator (home tool: roster + innings to inning-by-inning chart); tball positions template / t-ball fielding rotation (10-12 fielders, no pitcher, everyone bats); fair playing time baseball lineup generator (no one sits twice before everyone sits once); softball defensive lineup generator by inning; printable baseball lineup card pdf (dugout card + blank card); youth baseball position rotation template for Little League-age teams (descriptive use only, never in title brand)
- **Risks:** Strongly seasonal (peaks Feb-June, with a smaller fall bump). 'Little League' is a registered trademark, so use it only descriptively in copy and never in the brand or subdomain. League rules vary (pitch counts, continuous batting), so present rules as user settings and never as official policy. The iOS app or GameChanger-style apps may rank on live Google for some variants. Spot-check the SERP manually before building.
- **Evidence checked:** (1) 'baseball fielding rotation generator': page 1 was the iOS Baseball Fielding Rotation App (apps.apple.com/hn/app/baseball-fielding-rotation-app/id1355733523, free tier capped at 7 innings, season totals paid), its mwm.ai and similarweb listings, and one farm (tech.mozilla.com.tw/en/baseball-fielding-lineup-template.html). No web generator. (2) 'free youth baseball lineup generator equal playing time positions by inning': farms (chw.dhis2.org, live.museum.manchester.ac.uk, mariadb3.chipta.com 'Little League Position Rotation Template'), Dugout Buddy app (sites.google.com/view/mrbaconcatapps-lineupbuddy), a free Payhip planner download (payhip.com/b/C6BQR), Play Ball Lineup iOS (apps.apple.com/us/app/id877233521) and LineupPro (mwm.ai). (3) 'online defensive lineup generator softball tball website': DugoutIQ (alternativeto.net/software/dugoutiq/about, described as a free web lineup app with a rotation engine; login status unverified), GameChanger 'Lineup Recommendations BETA' (help.gc.com/hc/en-us/articles/42067886646157, needs an account and at least one scored game), Who's on Second iOS (apps.apple.com/us/app/whos-on-second/id6761384188), Baseball Lineup iOS, and farms (my-test.valpo.edu, tech.mozilla.com.tw). None of the 3 searches showed a free, no-login web generator in the top results.

### WickLabel — build
A free candle and wax melt label maker. It pre-fills the standard cautionary warning text and lays it out on common round and rectangle label sheets, or on 4x6 thermal labels, as a print-aligned PDF.

- **Audience:** Home and small-batch candle, wax melt and reed diffuser makers who sell on Etsy, at craft fairs and in local shops (mostly US)
- **Keywords:** candle warning label template; free printable candle warning labels, wax melt warning label template, wax melt label template, candle warning label 2 inch round, candle safety label wording, candle label maker free
- **What we'd do better:** A real generator. You pick the product type (container candle, pillar, tealight, wax melt, reed diffuser) and get the commonly used cautionary wording pre-filled and editable (burn within sight, keep away from children and pets, and so on, with a heavy WARNING header). You add your business name, address, scent, net weight and logo, and can print a front label and a warning label as a set. The tool lays everything out precisely on common sheet sizes (2in round 12-up, 1.5in round 20-up, 2x3 10-up, 2x4) and on 4x6 thermal labels, with an alignment test page. Page 1 has only spam PDFs and Avery's account-oriented designer.
- **Output:** Print-aligned PDF label sheets (Letter/A4 and 4x6 thermal) and a PNG of a single label, carrying a 'Made with WickLabel' credit on the sheet margin
- **Fits the network:** Shares the label-sheet and thermal 4x6 layout code with LabelFit and PrepLabel. Same Etsy maker audience as CollabRate and InvoiceKit. Its engine could later power the cottage food label idea considered earlier.
- **Reviewer's call:** Many separate spam farms clone the candle, wax melt and printable variants. I found no real free generator, and the job is functional, not design-led (pre-filled cautionary wording laid out exactly on common round sheets or 4x6), which Canva and static PDFs handle badly. It reuses the LabelFit and PrepLabel sheet code. The main risks are OnlineLabels, Avery or Canva ranking on real Google, which I could not verify, and safety-wording liability, so frame the text as 'commonly used wording' and link out to ASTM F2058 and local rules.
- **First pages to build:** candle warning label 2 inch round template (12 per sheet, print-aligned); wax melt warning label template printable; reed diffuser warning label template; candle warning labels for 4x6 thermal printer; what to put on a candle warning label (commonly used wording, editable, links to ASTM F2058); candle jar label with scent, net weight and warning (front + back set)

Frame the text as commonly used wording and link out to the ASTM candle cautionary-labeling standard (F2058 is the one usually cited) and to local rules. Avery has strong domain authority and could rank its own page. Canva covers part of the need. Use is batch-based (every restock or new scent), not daily.

Outputs: a poster chart, half-page job cards with descriptions, name clips, and a projector-friendly 'this week' view the teacher opens each Monday, which drives weekly repeat visits. Themes and icons would be open-licensed.
- **Output:** A job chart poster PDF (letter and tabloid), job cards with descriptions, name clips or pocket cards, a full-year rotation schedule PDF, a full-screen 'this week' display, and the 'Made with' credit on printables
- **Fits the network:** Moderate with BellBook (the same K-12 teachers; A/B-day schools can rotate per cycle) and TicketRun (classroom reward tickets). Weak with GoalTally.
- **Reviewer's call:** Demand is large and recurring, with a Pinterest-heavy K-5 audience and weekly visits for the 'this week' display. The head term is held by small paid sellers rather than free tools, and the 'free printable' variants have spam on page 1. Its success depends almost entirely on design quality and openly licensed icons, so build it only with a real design pass.
- **First pages to build:** free printable classroom job chart; editable classroom jobs chart with student names; classroom jobs rotation schedule; classroom jobs list with descriptions printable; classroom job cards printable; preschool classroom job chart with pictures
- **Risks:** Teachers pick on looks, and TPT and Canva sellers set a high design bar, so themes and icons must look great and be licensed. Strong seasonal peak in August-September with a smaller January bump. The head term is likely owned by Canva, TPT and Pinterest, so success depends on the rotation and generator long-tail. Student names stay local, which is fine.

### TabFlyer — build
Type your service and phone number once and get a print-ready flyer with 8-12 rotated tear-off tabs, dashed cut lines and an optional QR code on every tab.

- **Audience:** Tutors, dog walkers, babysitters, house cleaners, lawn care and handyman solo operators, music teachers, people renting rooms; mostly US, posting on community boards, laundromats, libraries and coffee shops
- **Keywords:** tear off flyer template; tear off flyer template free, flyer with tear off tabs, tear off flyer template google docs, business flyer with tear off tabs, tutoring flyer with tear off tabs, dog walking flyer tear off tabs
- **What we'd do better:** A purpose-built generator. You enter headline, blurb, price and contact once, and it repeats the contact text, rotated, on every tab. You choose the tab count (6-14), add an optional QR code per tab and get dashed cut lines. It can put 1-up or 2-up flyers on a Letter or A4 sheet. It ships audience presets (tutor, dog walker, cleaner, lawn care, babysitter, room for rent, lost pet) and has one landing page per preset. It exports a vector PDF and PNG with no sign-up, which Canva, edit.org and Wepik all require for some features.
- **Output:** Print-ready PDF (Letter/A4) plus PNG for sharing; small 'Made with TabFlyer' credit in the margin
- **Fits the network:** TicketRun's cut-line and multi-up print layout code. The audiences (tutors, dog walkers) also overlap with the dog walk report card idea and with GradLedger's homeschool/tutor visitors.
- **Reviewer's call:** This is a real, steady US need for many solo service providers. The only legitimate competitors are a static Word file (Vertex42) and editors where you fill each tab by hand.
