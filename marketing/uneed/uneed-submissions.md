## 2. GiftStub

- **Name:** GiftStub
- **Website:** https://giftcert.roohsites.com
- **Tagline (short, 50 chars):** Printable gift certificates with numbered stubs
- **Tagline (long, ~105 chars):** Print numbered gift certificates with matching stubs, 3 per page. Free, no signup, nothing uploaded.
- **Short description (~200 chars):**
  Make printable gift certificates where every certificate has a unique number and a matching stub for your records. Presets for salons, spas, photographers and more. Letter or A4 PDF, free.
- **Long description:**
  GiftStub is a free gift certificate maker for small businesses that need numbered certificates. Each one carries its own number, and a matching stub with "Issued to / Date / Amount / Redeemed" lines stays with you.

  Pick a preset (General, Salon, Massage & spa, Photography, Bakery, Auto detailing or Holiday) and add your business name, logo, value and terms. Then choose a prefix and start number and print up to 500 certificates at once, 3 per US Letter or A4 page.

  You can also download a single certificate as an image to email to a customer. A simple issue-and-redeem log, exportable as CSV, helps you track which numbers were sold and used.

  Everything runs in your browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. The expiry date is off by default, because gift certificate rules vary by country and US state.

- **Tags:** gift certificate template, numbered gift certificates, gift voucher, salon, spa, small business, printable
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Maker / company:** Rooh Sites (same links as above)
