Privacy

InvoiceKit runs in your browser. Invoice content, clients and your logo are stored in your browser's local storage and are never sent to our servers. When you activate Pro, your license key is sent to our server once to verify it with our payment provider, Gumroad. Payments are processed by Gumroad, and we never see your card details.
