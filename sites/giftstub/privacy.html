Privacy & Terms

Short version: your certificates, logo and log never leave your device.

Privacy

GiftStub runs entirely in your browser. Certificate wording, numbers, logos and log entries are processed on your device to build the preview, the PDF, the PNG and the CSV files. They are never sent to our servers or to anyone else.

To save you retyping, the tool remembers your last certificate and your logo in this browser's local storage. The issue and redeem log is stored there too. Clearing your browser's site data deletes all of it, so export the log CSV if you want to keep it.

We use two privacy-friendly analytics services, Vercel Web Analytics and Umami, to count page views and a few anonymous events, such as “PDF downloaded” with a rough size bucket. Neither uses cookies or tracks you across sites, and neither ever receives what you type into the tool.

Terms

GiftStub is provided “as is”, without warranty. Check your certificates before printing a full run. You're responsible for the certificates you issue, the promises written on them and following the rules that apply to your business. GiftStub is free for personal and commercial use.
