Why these tools are different

  • Private by design. There is no server-side processing. Your files, photos and records never leave your device, which matters for buyers' addresses, student data and client details.
  • Free, with no catch. Every feature is free. There are no accounts, trials or upgrade walls. Exports carry a small credit, which is how other people find the tools.
  • Made for one job. Each tool has presets and guides for its exact use: carrier label sizes, Amazon label formats, ABA graphing conventions, A/B rotation rules and GPA weighting.
  • Fast on any device. No frameworks, no ads in the tools, and several of them work offline after the first visit.

How to pick the right tool

Start from the job, not the tool: if you sell online and print labels, LabelFit fixes carrier labels and PrepLabel makes Amazon FNSKU labels. Teachers will find GoalTally for ABA data, BellBook for rotation calendars and GradLedger for homeschool transcripts. Small businesses can make invoices with InvoiceKit and numbered gift certificates with GiftStub; landlords and contractors can document jobs with Roomproof and label electrical panels with PanelDex; creators can price brand deals with CollabRate and post stream schedules with StreamWeek. Each tool's home page has a short guide, presets for common cases and an FAQ, so you can get a correct result on the first try.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool and every feature is free, with no account and no paid tier. Exports carry a small “Made with …” credit.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs entirely in your browser. Invoices, labels, photos, student records and schedules stay on your device and are never sent to our servers.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. Open a tool in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone and start using it. Several tools also work offline after the first visit.
