About InvoiceKit

Small, fast tools that do one job well, free, with no accounts and no tracking of your data.

InvoiceKit is part of MiniTools, a set of free single-purpose web tools for freelancers and small businesses. Each tool runs entirely in your browser: what you type stays on your device, and nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Why we built it

Most invoice apps want an account, a monthly subscription and your client list before you can send a single invoice. We think a freelancer sending three invoices a month deserves a tool that just works. Every feature of InvoiceKit is free, with no paid tier and no watermark.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free today: logo, saved clients, history and all designs. There are no ads in the tool, and we never sell data, partly because we never have it.
