Which invoice do I need in India?

Your situationDocument
Registered for GST and charging GSTGST tax invoice
Not registered for GSTInvoice without GST
Registered composition taxpayer, or selling exempt itemsBill of supply
Registered and billing a client abroad without IGSTExport invoice under LUT

Rules differ by business and change over time, so confirm with a chartered accountant or on the GST portal.

Why use an online invoice generator instead of Word or Excel?

Spreadsheets break when you add rows, and Word templates don't calculate totals. A generator handles the maths, keeps invoice numbers in sequence, and gives you a clean PDF every time. InvoiceKit runs entirely in your browser, so your client data stays private.
