About PriceBoard

A small, free tool that does one job: clear price lists and service menus for salons, barbers, nail studios and spas, for print and for Instagram.

PriceBoard is part of Rooh Sites' collection of free single-purpose web tools. It is built for hair salons, barbershops, nail salons, spas and massage therapists, lash and brow artists and estheticians who need a price list that looks professional and is quick to update.

Why we built it

Most price list templates online are fixed designs in an editor that wants an account, or paid files that break as soon as your list is longer than the sample. PriceBoard starts from the list instead of the design: you type services and prices, and it works out the text size, the columns and the alignment so the whole menu fits one page, in print sizes and in image sizes for Instagram, stories and TV screens.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no account and no paid tier. Printed PDFs carry one small “Made with PriceBoard” line at the bottom of the page; images for Instagram, stories and TV screens have none. That line is how other business owners find the tool.

How it works

Everything runs in your browser. PDFs are built on your device with the open-source pdf-lib library, QR codes are drawn with the open-source qrcode-generator library, and your list and logo are kept in your browser's local storage. Nothing you type is uploaded.

PriceBoard is independent and isn't affiliated with Canva, Adobe, any booking app or any print shop. Sample businesses, phone numbers and prices are made up to show the layout. Our guides are general information, not financial, tax or legal advice.
