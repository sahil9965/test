Facials, peels and waxing on one page

An esthetician's menu often mixes a few long treatments with many short ones. The facials take a line each, with the length or the main benefit in a note, while waxing is a long list of short areas and prices. PriceBoard handles both on one page: it balances the sections across columns, so the waxing list doesn't push the facials off the page.

If your treatment names come from a product line, add a few plain words in the note so a new client knows what to expect: “60 minutes, for dry and dull skin” or “Gentle exfoliation, no downtime”.

The fine print that saves appointments

Prep rules matter more for skin and waxing than for most services. A client who used a retinol last night may have to be turned away, so print the rule where they will see it. Good candidates for the fine print are retinol and exfoliant rules, hair length for waxing, sun exposure before a peel, and your cancellation policy. Keep it short; the QR code can open the full details.

Where to use it

  • Treatment room: Letter or A4 in a frame, or Half Letter on a stand.
  • Front desk: a 5 × 7 in sign holder with your most-booked services.
  • Online: the square post and the story size for Instagram, and the PNG on your booking profile.

Write 45+ for a starting price; PriceBoard prints it as $45+. Anything that isn't a plain number, like “from $45”, “$90 / 60 min” or “Free”, prints exactly as you type it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I lay out a long waxing menu?

Give waxing its own section and list areas from face down to legs, or from most to least booked. With dot leaders on, a long list of short names and prices is easy to scan, and PriceBoard puts it in its own column when there's room.

Should I list face and body waxing separately?

If your waxing menu is long, two sections (“Face waxing” and “Body waxing”) make it quicker to find an area. For seven or eight areas, one section is enough.

Where do I put prep and aftercare rules?

Short prep rules, such as stopping retinol before waxing or a peel, fit in the fine print. Longer aftercare belongs on your booking page; the QR code at the bottom can open it.

Can I mark which facials need a consultation?

Yes, with a short note under the service, for example “Consultation required for first visit”.
