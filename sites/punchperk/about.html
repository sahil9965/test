About PunchPerk

A small, free tool that does one job: printable loyalty punch cards, 10 to a page, ready for a home printer.

PunchPerk is part of Rooh Sites' collection of free single-purpose web tools. It is built for coffee carts, food trucks, cafés, salons, barbers, car washes, home bakers, dog groomers and boba stands who want cheap paper loyalty cards, and for teachers and parents who want behavior, reading and chore cards.

Why we built it

Most punch card templates online are single images or documents that you have to copy, line up and number yourself, and most design editors want an account and make one card at a time. PunchPerk fills the gap: you design a card once, and get a PDF with 10 real business-card-size cards per sheet, optional serial numbers and matching backs that line up when printed two-sided.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no account and no paid tier. Each printed sheet has a small “Made with PunchPerk” line in the bottom margin, outside the cards and the crop marks. It never appears on a card or in the PNG image. That's how other business owners find the tool.

How it works

Everything runs in your browser. PDFs are built on your device with the open-source pdf-lib library, the QR code on the back is made with the open-source qrcode-generator library, and your settings and logo are kept in your browser's local storage. Nothing you type is uploaded.

PunchPerk is independent and isn't affiliated with Avery, Canva, Microsoft, Google, Apple or any print shop. Where we say a layout is compatible with common business card sheets, we mean the card size and margins match. Our guides are general information, not legal, tax or accounting advice.
