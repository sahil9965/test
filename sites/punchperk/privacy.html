Privacy & Terms

Short version: your cards, logo and settings never leave your device.

Privacy

PunchPerk runs entirely in your browser. Card wording, serial numbers, logos and QR links are processed on your device to build the preview, the PDF, the PNG and the alignment test. They are never sent to our servers or to anyone else.

To save you retyping, the tool remembers your last card and your logo in this browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, and the Reset button clears it too. A very large logo is used for the visit but not saved.

We use one privacy-friendly analytics service, Umami, to count page views and a few anonymous events, such as “PDF downloaded” with the sheet layout and number of cards. It does not use cookies or track you across sites, and it never receives what you type into the tool.

Terms

PunchPerk is provided “as is”, without warranty. Check a test print before you print a full run. You are responsible for the cards you issue, the promises written on them and following the rules that apply to your business, including any rules on loyalty program expiry and terms. Serial numbers and staff initials help you track cards but are not fraud-proof. PunchPerk is free for personal and commercial use.
