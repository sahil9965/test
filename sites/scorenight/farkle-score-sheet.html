Farkle Score Sheet (Free Printable)

A turn-by-turn Farkle sheet for 2 to 8 players with Score and Total columns, your entry points and winning total at the top, and the scoring chart on the same page.

Quick answer: The Farkle sheet above prints one row per turn with a Score and a Total column for each player, and the scoring chart under the table when you print one sheet per page. Set the points to get on the board (500 is common) and the points to win (10,000 is common), type the names and download the PDF.

What the sheet prints

The top of the sheet shows the game name, a date line, your game night name and your two key rules, for example “Get on the board: 500 · Win: 10,000”. Below that, each player has two columns: Score for the points banked that turn and Total for the running total. A one-per-page sheet holds up to 30 turns and the scoring chart; two to a page holds 15 turns each without the chart.

Writing the running total each turn means nobody has to add up a long column at the end, and it's easy to see when someone is close to the winning total.

How a turn works

  1. Roll all six dice.
  2. Set aside at least one scoring die or combination.
  3. Stop and bank the turn's points, or roll the remaining dice to add more.
  4. If a roll has no scoring dice, that's a farkle: the turn scores 0.
  5. If all six dice score, you have hot dice and can roll all six again.

A player's points don't count until they bank the entry minimum in one turn. When someone reaches the winning total, everyone else gets one last turn, and the highest total wins.

Frequently asked questions

How many points do you need to get on the board in Farkle?

Many families play 500: a player's first score only counts once they bank at least 500 in a single turn. Some play 350 or 1,000, and some have no minimum at all. Set yours under Points to get on the board; 0 means no minimum.

What score do you play Farkle to?

10,000 is the most common target. When a player reaches it, everyone else gets one more turn to beat that total. For a shorter game, play to 5,000.

What is a farkle?

A roll with no scoring dice. The player loses every point from that turn and the dice pass to the next player. On the sheet, write 0 for the turn.

What are hot dice?

If every one of the six dice scores, you can pick them all up and roll all six again, adding to the same turn's points. Many tables require you to roll again after hot dice.

Do three farkles in a row cost points?

Under some house rules, yes: three farkles in a row lose 1,000 points. It isn't universal. The phone scorer doesn't apply it, so subtract the penalty by hand if your table uses it.

Is three 1s worth 1,000 or 300?

Both are common. Choose under Three 1s score; the chart on the sheet prints your choice. See the Farkle scoring chart for the full table.
