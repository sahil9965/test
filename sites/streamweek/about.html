About StreamWeek

A small, free tool that does one job: a weekly stream schedule with the times converted for your viewers.

StreamWeek is part of Rooh Sites' collection of free single-purpose web tools. It's built for streamers and VTubers who post a schedule every week on X, Discord and Instagram and have viewers in more than one time zone.

Why we built it

Schedule templates make good-looking images, but they leave the hard part to you: working out what 8 PM your time is in Tokyo or New York, in the weeks when daylight saving shifts everything by an hour. StreamWeek does that conversion from real dates, then turns one form into images, Discord timestamps, X text and a calendar file.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no account and no paid tier. Each exported image has a small “Made with StreamWeek · streamschedule.roohsites.com” credit in a bottom corner, away from your schedule. That's how other streamers find the tool. The copied text and the .ics file have no credit.

How it works

Everything runs in your browser. Time zones come from your browser's built-in time-zone database, images are drawn on a canvas on your device with the open-source Inter typeface, and your schedule is saved in local storage. Nothing you type or upload is sent to us.

StreamWeek is an independent tool, not affiliated with Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Discord, X, Instagram or TikTok. Platform names are used only to describe what the tool works with.
