Privacy & Terms

Short version: your schedule and pictures never leave your device.

Privacy

StreamWeek runs entirely in your browser. Stream titles, times, time zones and any avatar, logo or model art you add are processed on your device to build the preview, the PNG images, the copied text and the .ics file. They are never sent to our servers or to anyone else.

To save you retyping, the tool remembers your schedule and your picture in this browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and a few anonymous events, such as “PNG downloaded” with the size and theme. It's cookie-free, doesn't track you across sites and never receives what you type into the tool.

Terms

StreamWeek is provided “as is”, without warranty. Check your schedule before posting. You're responsible for what you publish and for having the rights to any picture you add. StreamWeek is free for personal and commercial use.
