Twitch Schedule Template

Make a weekly schedule image for socials, a narrow panel for your channel page and a Discord post, from one form.

Quick answer: Fill in your week below, then pick a size: 1920 × 1080 for X and Discord, 1080 × 1920 for Stories, or the 320-px-wide panel for your channel's About section, whose height grows with your week. Times show in your zone and up to 3 viewer zones, and Copy for Discord gives timestamps every viewer sees in their own time.

Three places your schedule lives

Most streamers end up posting the schedule in three places: an image on social media, a panel on the channel page and an announcement in Discord. Keeping three copies in sync by hand is where mistakes creep in, especially when you change one stream mid-week. Here, one form feeds all of them: the PNG sizes, the Discord timestamp text, the X text and the .ics calendar file.

The panel version

The panel layout is a narrow, stacked list 320 pixels wide. Each day is a row with the day and date on the left and the streams on the right. Offline days collapse to a single short line, so a three-stream week makes a short panel and a busy week a taller one. The title, your handle and the week's dates sit at the top, and the zone line lists which zones the times are in. Re-export it each week, or keep a general version with TBA days if your schedule rarely changes.

Retro arcade, or any other theme

This page starts with the Retro arcade theme: chunky yellow borders, hard shadows in your accent colour and a checker strip along the edges. If it's too loud for your channel, switch to Minimal dark or Clean light. Every theme takes an accent colour, so you can match your overlay or emotes rather than any platform's colours.

Times for viewers in other countries

Viewers abroad often lurk because they can't tell when you're live. Pick the 2 or 3 zones your channel analytics show most, and each stream gets a line per zone: “4:00 PM EDT”, “5:00 AM JST +1”. The conversion uses the real date, so it stays right across daylight-saving changes. See the time-zone guide for the weeks that usually go wrong.

A weekly routine that takes two minutes

  1. Open StreamWeek; last week's schedule is still there.
  2. Click Next week. Every stream moves forward 7 days with the same times.
  3. Change the titles and any days that differ.
  4. Download the 1920 × 1080 image and the panel, and copy the Discord text.
  5. Post, pin and update your channel's built-in schedule if you use it.

Frequently asked questions

Does Twitch have its own schedule feature?

Yes, channels can add stream segments to a built-in schedule on their channel page. Many streamers also post a schedule image on social media and in Discord, which is what this template is for. You can use both.

What size is the panel image?

320 pixels wide, a width commonly used for channel About panels. The height fits your week: offline days take a short row and busy days take more.

Can I show categories?

Yes. The game or category field appears as a small tag under each title, for example Just Chatting, Minecraft or Art.

Will the image look right in dark mode?

The image carries its own background, so it looks the same on light and dark pages. Midnight neon, Retro arcade and Minimal dark suit dark channel pages; Clean light and Pastel idol stand out on light ones.

Is this an official Twitch template?

No. StreamWeek is an independent free tool and isn't affiliated with Twitch. It uses no platform logos or brand colours.
