Raffle Tickets 1-500, Printable with Stubs

Five hundred numbered tickets with matching stubs on 50 sheets. The usual size for a school, church or club fundraiser raffle.

Quick answer: Raffle tickets 1-500 print on 50 sheets at 10 per page (or 63 sheets at 8 per page). The generator above is already set to 001–500: change the event name, prize, date and price, then click Download PDF and print at 100% scale.

500 tickets is the most common size for a school, church or club fundraiser. It prints on 50 sheets at 10 per page, which is a tenth of a standard 500-sheet ream, and fits in one envelope box when cut.

Cutting 500 tickets quickly

At this size, cutting sheet by sheet gets slow. Choose Number order: Cut-and-stack, print all 50 sheets, keep them in order and cut the whole stack along the crop marks (or in a few smaller stacks, keeping them in order, if your cutter can't take 50 sheets of cardstock). You end up with 10 piles of 50 tickets: 001–050, 051–100 and so on, each already in sequence.

Selling 500 tickets with volunteers

Twenty books of 25 or fifty books of 10 are both common. Books of 10 suit sellers who sell to family and friends; books of 25 suit people selling at a stall. The seller log prints the range of every book and has columns for sold, returned and cash, so you can see straight away if a book is short.

Print and cut

  1. Print the Test sheet on plain paper at 100% / Actual size and check that the crop marks line up.
  2. Print the full PDF (in batches if your printer prefers) on light cardstock.
  3. Cut along the crop marks: with cut-and-stack order, cut the whole stack at once; one cut down the middle and four across at 10 per page.
  4. Fold along the dashed line so the stub tears off cleanly when you sell each ticket.

Check your local raffle rules. Raffle and 50/50 laws vary by state, province and country: some require a licence or limit raffles to registered charities and nonprofits, and some restrict prices, prizes or online sales. This page is general information, not legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

How many sheets do I need for 500 raffle tickets?

50 sheets at 10 per page, or 63 sheets at 8 per page (the last sheet has 4 tickets). Add a few spares for misprints.

Can I print 1-500 in two colours?

Yes. Download 1–250 in one accent colour and 251–500 in another, or use two prefixes such as R- and B-.

How long does the PDF take?

A few seconds. The PDF is built in your browser and downloads straight away.
